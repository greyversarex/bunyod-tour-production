Our partners are divided into two groups: strategic partners and travel partners. We collaborate with strategic partners in a conventional manner, but we primarily engage with travel partners, also referred to as travel agencies, via online platforms, particularly our website. We offer a commission of 10% of the cost of sold tour packages posted on our website at https://bunyodtour.tj to travel agents interested in becoming our travel partners. Please note that we do not have strict restrictions on the selection of travel agents. Any individual or legal entity that meets the basic criteria and requirements of a travel agent and resides or operates in the countries of Central Asia can become our travel partner.
